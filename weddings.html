Wedding Menu Options

Thoughtfully planned menus bring together regional Indian favorites and polished Western courses, with live stations and service styles tailored to your celebration.

Indian Menu Options

Vegetarian Highlights

  • Interactive Live Stations: Amritsari Kulcha with Chole counters, live Tawa Chaat, and stone-ground Dosa bars with customized chutneys.
  • Gourmet Mains: Rich classics like slow-cooked Dal Makhani, simmered overnight, Paneer Makhmali Tikka Masala, and Kashmiri Dum Aloo.
  • Regional Specialties: Rajasthani Gatte ki Sabzi or Gujarati Undhiyu served in traditional brass mini-vessels.

Non-Vegetarian Highlights

  • Live Grills & Kebabs: Charcoal-smoked Murgh Malai Tikka, Galouti Kebabs on micro-ulte tawa parathas, and Amritsari Fish Fry stations.
  • Heritage Mains: Rogan Josh, classic butter chicken, and slow-cooked Dum Lamb Biryani sealed with dough.
  • Coastal Additions: Goan Prawn Curry or Chicken Chettinad paired with appams or fragrant jeera rice.

Western Menu Options

Vegetarian Highlights

  • Appetizers & Small Plates: Artisanal burrata bars with heirloom tomatoes, truffle mushroom crostini, and wood-fired flatbreads with goat cheese and caramelized onions.
  • Plated or Buffet Mains: Butternut squash ravioli in sage brown butter sauce, seared wild-mushroom risotto, and cauliflower steaks with chimichurri and toasted pine nuts.

Non-Vegetarian Highlights

  • Appetizers & Small Plates: Prosciutto-wrapped melon, bacon-wrapped dates with balsamic glaze, and seared scallop spoons with lemon-herb butter.
  • Plated or Buffet Mains: Slow-roasted prime rib with red wine reduction, herb-crusted rack of lamb, and pan-seared Atlantic salmon with a dill-cream emulsion.

Seating Arrangements & Layouts

Every layout is shaped around the ceremony, the people who matter most, and the way guests will move through the celebration.

Indian Wedding Seating Layouts

The Ceremony Core: Mandap & Ritual Zones

  • Elders' Zone: Dedicated, comfortable sofa-style seating in the front rows, directly flanking or facing the mandap so grandparents and senior relatives remain close to the rituals.
  • Family-Side Balancing: Clear structural divisions for Ladwale and Ladkiwale blocks during sacred ceremonies, transitioning into blended seating for post-ceremony celebrations.

The Reception Layout

  • The Elevated Couple's Stage: Ornate throne-style chairs or a floral couch on a central stage for visibility, guest greetings, and photography.
  • Tiered Table Clustering: Large 8-to-10-seater round tables with immediate family closest to the stage, spreading outward to extended relatives and friends.

Western Wedding Seating Layouts

The Ceremony Layout

  • Modern Minimalist Rows: Clean, straight theatre-style chair rows with an open center aisle, floral aisle markers, or texture-rich ribbons instead of heavy bows.

The Reception Layout

  • Long Banquet Tables: Grand, uninterrupted linear wooden tables with low-profile floral runners and ambient candlelight for an intimate, communal European dining aesthetic.
  • Cocktail & Lounge Pockets: High cocktail tables interspersed with modular velvet sofas and low coffee tables near the bar to encourage fluid guest mingling.

Comprehensive & Versatile Wedding Drinks Menu

Traditional Desi Coolers & Energy Refreshers

  • Chilled Sattu Drinks: Roasted chana sattu in sweet variations with jaggery, lemon, and mint, or salted with roasted cumin and black salt.
  • Fresh Shakes & Smoothies: Seasonal mango shakes made with fresh pulp, banana-caramel smoothies, and mixed-fruit smoothies.
  • Natural Juices: Chilled watermelon juice, fresh sweet lime mosambi juice, and tender coconut water served straight from the shell.
  • Everyday Classics: Salted or sweet chaas seasoned with coriander, Shikanji lemonade, and spiced Jaljeera.

Trendy Mocktails & Fruit Bar

  • Mint Mojito Station: Virgin mojitos muddled with mint, lime, sugar cane, and club soda, with strawberry or passionfruit purees.
  • Virgin Sangria: Mixed fruit juices, chopped seasonal fruits, cinnamon, and ginger ale.
  • Sunset Punch: Layered orange juice, cranberry juice, and grenadine.
  • Cucumber-Basil Cooler: Fresh cucumber ribbons, sweet basil, lime juice, and sparkling soda water.

Carbonated & Fizzy Soda Bar

  • Classic Fizzes: Chilled Coca-Cola, Thums Up, Sprite, Fanta, and Diet Coke.
  • Artisanal Fizzy Flavors: Ginger Ale, Tonic Water, Bitter Lemon, and premium bottled mixers.
  • Desi Banta/Soda Cart: Street-style lemon sodas in pressurized glass bottles with black salt and chaat masala, in Jeera, Masala, and Kala Khatta flavors.

Hot Beverage & Interactive Stations

  • Kulhad Chai & Filter Coffee Bar: Cardamom and ginger tea with loose leaves alongside frothy South Indian filter coffee, served in clay kulhads.
  • Gourmet Coffee Cart: Barista-style iced lattes, hot cappuccinos, and vanilla, hazelnut, or caramel syrups.

On-Demand Guest Additions

  • Custom Mocktail Barista Station: Guests choose their fruit base, syrup, and herbs for a custom mocktail.
  • Detox & Warm Water Station: Lemon-cucumber-mint and strawberry-basil spa waters, plus warm boiled water for elderly guests and winter setups.
